📖 Clinical Features Reference Dictionary

Feature Code Clinical Parameter Units / Encoding Reference Range Diagnostic Significance
age Patient Age Years 29 - 77 years Atherosclerosis and coronary calcification incidence increase progressively with age.
sex Biological Sex 1 = Male, 0 = Female Binary Estrogen offers premenopausal vascular protection; males historically present earlier.
cp Chest Pain Type 0: Typical Angina, 1: Atypical, 2: Non-Anginal, 3: Asymptomatic Categorical (0-3) Gold standard symptom classifier for myocardial ischemia localization.
trestbps Resting Blood Pressure mm Hg < 120 mm Hg (Normal) Sustained systemic hypertension damages endothelial vascular lining.
chol Serum Cholesterol mg/dL < 200 mg/dL Atherogenic LDL fraction leads to arterial wall plaque accumulation.
fbs Fasting Blood Sugar > 120 1 = True, 0 = False < 100 mg/dL Markers for diabetes mellitus, an independent vascular risk accelerator.
restecg Resting Electrocardiography 0: Normal, 1: ST-T wave abnormality, 2: LV Hypertrophy Categorical (0-2) Detects baseline myocardial strain, ischemia, or chamber enlargement.
thalach Maximum Heart Rate Beats Per Minute (bpm) Chronotropic response Chronotropic incompetence (inability to reach age-predicted max HR) indicates ischemic burden.
exang Exercise-Induced Angina 1 = Yes, 0 = No Binary Strong indicator of hemodynamically significant coronary luminal stenosis.
oldpeak ST Depression mm 0.0 - 6.2 mm Direct electrocardiographic correlate of subendocardial myocardial ischemia during exertion.
slope Peak Exercise ST Slope 0: Upsloping, 1: Flat, 2: Downsloping Categorical (0-2) Downsloping and horizontal ST segments correlate with severe multi-vessel disease.
ca Vessels Colored by Fluoroscopy 0 - 3 major vessels 0 (Normal) Direct visualization of calcified coronary arteries via fluoroscopy.
thal Thallium Scintigraphy 1: Normal, 2: Fixed Defect, 3: Reversible Defect Categorical (1-3) Nuclear imaging delineating reversible ischemia versus permanent infarction scar tissue.
